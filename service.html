OUR STORY

About Style Nest

Style Nest is a sample women's fashion storefront created as a learning project. Explore the collection, add dresses to a browser-based cart, and try a checkout demonstration. It is not a live store.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

Add a dress to your cart, continue to delivery details, and choose one of the demonstration payment methods. No real order is placed.

How can I select a dress size?

Choose a size from the product card or open View Details to select a size and colour before adding the dress.

How can I contact customer support?

Use the demo contact form below. It displays a confirmation in your browser and does not send your message.

How can I cancel a demonstration order?

The checkout does not send an order to a store, so there is no real order to cancel. The cart is cleared after the demo confirmation.
